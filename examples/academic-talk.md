## Does installing rooftop solar change how much electricity households use?
- Rebound effect: cheaper self-generated power may raise consumption
- Matters for grid planning and net-zero targets
Notes: State the research question up front.

## Prior studies disagree on whether a solar rebound effect exists
- Some find consumption rises after installation
- Others find no change once weather is controlled for
Source: (Example & Author, 2021); (Sample et al., 2022)

## Difference-in-differences compares adopters with matched non-adopters
- Monthly smart-meter data for matched households
- Matched on pre-period usage, dwelling type and region
- Identifying assumption: parallel pre-trends

- Rebound exists but is small relative to generation
- Policy: solar subsidies deliver most of their expected grid savings
